You'll also need a key.properties file (NOT committed to git) at
android/key.properties:

    storePassword=<your-store-password>
    keyPassword=<your-key-password>
    keyAlias=<your-key-alias>
    storeFile=<path-to-your-.jks-keystore>

Generate the keystore once with:
    keytool -genkey -v -keystore ~/quetqrtpro-release.jks -keyalg RSA \
      -keysize 2048 -validity 10000 -alias quetqrtpro

Add `android/key.properties` and `*.jks` to .gitignore.
